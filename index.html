What is Ocean Pollution?

Ocean Pollution is the collection of trash, oil, chemicals and other foreign debris in the ocean. It collects over time eventually damaging sealife and the ecosystem. It is directly caused by human waste and improper means of disposal. Check out the History page for more!

Why is it important?

It is important to know and spread awareness about ocean pollution to help rally efforts to stop ocean pollution. This is extremely important as the ecosystems and wildlife are more and more endangered by pollution every day. Check out the Current News page for more!

What can I do about it?

You can help by joining local efforts, donating to causes and organizations and offering volunteer work. There are also smaller ways we can all help in our daily lives. Check out the Take Action page for more!
