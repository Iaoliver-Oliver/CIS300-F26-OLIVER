What Can I do at home?

Start with the basics: reduce your use of single-use plastics, properly dispose of waste, and conserve water at home. You can also drive slower to reduce gas emissions, carpool, and use public transportation.


Take it up a level!

If you really want to take it to the next level, consider volunteering for a local cleanup, donating to an ocean conservation organization, or advocating for stronger environmental protections in your community.
