What is Ocean Pollution?

Ocean Pollution is the collection of trash, oil, chemicals and other foreign debris in the ocean. It collects over time eventually damaging sealife and the ecosystem. It is a growing problem and if nothing is done about it our oceans will soon be uninhabitable.


What Causes it?

It is directly caused by human waste and improper means of disposal. Human activity causes pollution through industrial waste, plastic, and other debris that enters the ocean. It is also impacted by climate change which is caused by greenhouse gas emissions. Over time, this pollution damages marine life and ecosystems.


How did it come to be?

Humans have been polluting the earth since the dawn of time. In the beginning humans could only produce within their abilities to make things. So large scale production was nearly impossible and almost all goods were local. Due to this it also took a long time for things of value to be made so there wasn't an excess of purchasing happening either. After the industrial revolution things changed. Humans started producing on a scale much larger than they could handle. Thus their waste that had grown so rapidly had no place to go. It ended up in landfills, other countries and the ocean. This was expedited even further with the invention on plastic. This never degrading substance wouldn't only pollute the ocean but live there forever and never break down. This has all created the problem we all know of today as ocean pollution.


History of Ocean Plastic Accumulation

Over the years, the amount of plastic accumulating in the ocean has grown significantly. Below is a summary of the estimated total floating or accumulated ocean plastic over time starting from the year 2000. The reason is because major pollution didn't begin until roughly the 1950's with the invention of plastic. The 1950's-2000 was used as a baseline to determine the number of plastic in the ocean. Where are we at now?

This problem is still ongoing. Many organizations have started cleanup efforts as well as efforts to raise awareness about this topic. However, much more needs to be done to effectively combat ocean pollution and protect our marine ecosystems. There is currently a large amount of pollution in our oceans, microplastics taking over, oil infecting our waters, carbon emissions killing our coral reefs and a giant patch of collected trash floating out in the ocean like an island. For more news on the current state of our oceans check the Current News Page!


What can we do about it?

There are lots of ways to make a lasting impact on ocean pollution! You can start by reducing your use of single-use plastics, cutting down on green house gasses by driving slower and carpooling, properly disposing of waste, and participating in local cleanup efforts. Supporting organizations that work to protect our oceans and raising awareness in your community are also effective ways to contribute to the fight against ocean pollution. Every small action helps make a difference! For more information, check out the Take Action page!
